# Fuel Flow Pro Landing

Design a modern, premium landing page for Fuel Flow Pro, a petrol station management SaaS platform built for the Kenyan market. Use a dark, sleek theme (charcoal/black background) matching the brand logo — a cream/gold fuel pump icon inside a thin blue-bordered square, with the wordmark “Fuel Flow Pro” in elegant gold-gradient script and the tagline “Convenience at Fingertips” in light gray small caps beneath it.

Hero section: Full logo lockup centered or left-aligned, bold headline (“The Complete Fuel Station Management System”), subheadline emphasizing real-time control, M-Pesa payments, and multi-branch visibility, with a primary CTA (“Get Started” / “Book a Demo”) and secondary CTA (“See Pricing”).

Sections to include: Key Benefits (grid/cards), How It Works (3–4 step flow), Integrations (M-Pesa, banks, eTIMS/KRA), Multi-Branch Dashboard showcase (screenshot/mockup), Staff & Attendance management feature, Pricing tiers (Standard vs Premium with eTIMS), Testimonials/social proof, FAQ, Footer with contact and social links.

Style: Gold accents on dark background, clean sans-serif for body copy paired with the script logo font for headings, subtle glow/gradient effects echoing the logo’s blue border, mobile-responsive, fast-loading, trust-building tone for station owners.

Benefits List

Payments & Finance

Accept M-Pesa payments directly at the pump — no manual reconciliation

Integrated with all major banks for seamless transfers and settlements

Reduces cash handling risk and pilferage at stations

Compliance & Tax (Premium)

eTIMS integration for automatic, KRA-compliant e-invoicing

Cuts manual tax filing work and reduces compliance errors

Available as a premium subscription feature for stations that need it

Multi-Branch Management

Manage unlimited stations/branches from a single dashboard

Compare performance across branches in real time

Centralized control for owners with growing networks — no need to visit each site

Staff & Operations

Track staff attendance and shift activity remotely

View each attendant’s daily sales performance individually

Spot discrepancies or underperformance quickly

Analytics & Insights

Real-time sales and fuel volume analytics

Historical trends to guide pricing and restocking decisions

Custom reports for owners, managers, and accountants

General

Reduces revenue leakage and manual errors

Saves time on admin, reporting, and reconciliation

Built specifically for the realities of the Kenyan fuel retail market
